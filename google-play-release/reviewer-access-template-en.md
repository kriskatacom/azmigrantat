# Google Play reviewer access template

Do not submit this template until the placeholders are replaced with a permanent
review account that does not require OTP, device approval, or access to a
personal mailbox/phone.

## Sign-in instructions

1. Open the app and tap **Вход** (Sign in).
2. Enter the following reusable credentials:
   - Email: `[REVIEW_ACCOUNT_EMAIL]`
   - Password: `[REVIEW_ACCOUNT_PASSWORD]`
3. No one-time password, phone verification, location restriction, or previous
   device approval is required for this account.

## Feature access

- Chat: open **Съобщения**, select `[SECOND_TEST_ACCOUNT_NAME]`, and send a
  message.
- Audio/video call: open the same user's profile or chat and use the call
  control. A second maintained test account/device must be online.
- Video upload: open the video upload action, choose a non-sensitive test video,
  select a category, and submit it.
- Account deletion: open **Профил > Настройки > Изтриване на профила**. Type
  `delete account` and enter the current password. Do not complete this step on
  the shared reviewer account unless a disposable reviewer account is supplied.

## Notes for review

- Camera and microphone permissions are requested only when the reviewer opens
  a feature that needs them.
- Notification permission is requested after sign-in so the app can deliver
  chat and incoming-call notifications.
- `[ADD ANY GEO/ROLE/SUBSCRIPTION RESTRICTIONS OR STATE “NONE”]`

## Required maintenance

Keep the account active, reusable, and accessible worldwide for the entire
review period. Replace it immediately in Play Console if credentials change.
